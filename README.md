## 使用说明

- 银行账号、公司注册信息等公开资料只维护一个正式版本，其他页面只链接到这里。
- 文件修改应保留原因和历史，重要资料建议经过审核。
- 密码、API密钥、恢复码、私人身份证件和成员个人资料不得提交到 GitHub。
- 中文内容放在前面；英文技术目录和文件名继续保留，方便工具和多人协作。

## English

This public handbook is the shared source of truth for APOA's organizational knowledge and volunteer collaboration.

### Contents

- `about/` - organization profile, mission and structure
- `governance/` - registrations, policies and public decisions
- `finance/` - public payment information and finance processes
- `volunteers/` - onboarding, roles and handover guidance
- `community/` - community programs and public operating principles
- `meetings/` - approved agendas, minutes and decision records
- `events/` - public event plans, runbooks and retrospectives
- `operations/` - repeatable organizational workflows
- `registers/` - indexes of projects, assets and official accounts
- `templates/` - reusable document templates
