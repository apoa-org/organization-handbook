# APOA Lunar New Year and Lantern Festival Celebration

APOA held a free community cultural event celebrating Lunar New Year traditions, multicultural inclusion and community contribution.

## Event details

- Date: Sunday, 1 March 2026
- Venue: Botanic Cafe, 32 Jackson Street, Toorak, Victoria
- Program: Lantern Festival afternoon tea and annual Community Star Awards
- Audience: APOA members and the broader community
- Support: Victorian Government Multicultural Festivals and Events Program
