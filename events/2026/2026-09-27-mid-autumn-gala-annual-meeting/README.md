## 活动简介

这是 APOA 第三届中秋联欢暨年度会议。预计将有约 150-200 名维州物业持有者、行业专业人士、社区代表和受邀嘉宾参加。

APOA 是一个非营利组织，在维州拥有超过 4,000 名物业持有者及支持者组成的社区网络。本次活动旨在促进 APOA 成员、社区代表、行业人士和公共事务代表之间的相互了解，并就住房、租赁行业、物业投资以及其他影响维州的议题交换意见。

## English invitation letter

On behalf of the Australian Property Owners Alliance (APOA), I would be delighted to invite you to attend the **APOA Mid-Autumn Gala & Annual Meeting 2026**. We would also be most grateful if you could kindly extend this invitation to Mr. Matthew Guy MP.

The event is scheduled as follows:

- **Date:** Sunday, 27 September 2026
- **Time:** 1:30 pm-4:30 pm
- **Location:** 226 Kooyong Road, Toorak VIC 3142

This will be APOA's third annual Mid-Autumn Gala and Annual Meeting. We expect approximately 150-200 Victorian property owners, industry professionals, community representatives and invited guests to attend.

APOA is a not-for-profit organisation representing a network of more than 4,000 property owners and supporters across Victoria. We believe this event will provide a valuable opportunity for our members and representatives of the Victorian Liberal Party to develop greater mutual understanding and exchange views on housing, the rental sector, property investment and other matters affecting Victoria.

As part of the event program, we are also considering holding a moderated panel discussion on property, housing and broader issues facing Victoria. The format, topics and participants have not yet been finalised. If we decide to proceed, we will provide you with further information and discuss any potential participation separately.

As our planning progresses, we will send you further details, including the event program, invited guests and speaking arrangements.

We look forward to welcoming you.
